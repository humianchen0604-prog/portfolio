How hot is a memory?

Temperature is the time since anyone touched a memory. You or an agent bringing it up re-heats it. Left alone, it cools until it freezes. Memories you return to often cool more slowly.

The six cards

  • Molten: still heating, touched in the last day or so.
  • Simmering, then Cooling: losing heat over days and weeks.
  • Frosting over (icy edges), then Frozen: untouched for weeks, kept in ice.
  • Core memory: the one you keep coming back to.

Try

  • Swipe the tabs, from all memories to Interview, Family, Friends, Relationship and Money.
  • Drag the time bar, or press ▶, and watch the cards empty from Molten into Frozen.
  • Tap a card, then re-heat a memory to send it back to Molten.
  • Type in “Any bothers?” and press return. The new memory starts molten.
