How hot is a memory?

Temperature is the time since anyone touched it. You or an agent bringing it up re-heats it. Left alone, it cools and finally freezes. Memories you return to often cool more slowly.

Five states, named for the Lava icons

    How heat shows

    • Color follows the Thermal word's glow stack: cream → orange → ember, then dusk → blue → ice.
    • Shape: hot memories are liquid and wobble; frozen ones set into ice cubes with square corners.
    • Size: heat expands a memory and cold contracts it.
    • Surface: hot ones glow and throw off beads; cold ones frost over with a crystal hatch.
    • Place: memories rise from the Heat band into the frost (Lava Lamp), drift out from the core (Orbit), or press into thin layers like an ice core (Chain).
    • The whole space: the Heat band and Horizon glow dim, and the frost at the top spreads, as the average memory cools.
